About

Joe Suri

Founder, operator and dealmaker. I've been selling since I was fourteen, and I've spent most of my career inside parking and mobility: SKIDATA, then founding NextGen Mobility Reps, and today Square Parking and Park Equity.

Now I'm also building in EdTech. I co-founded ClearPath USA, which has served more than 50 international students and partners with Rutgers Camden.

Originally from England and based in New Jersey, I know what it takes to start over somewhere new. That UK and US perspective shapes how I build, sell and partner.
